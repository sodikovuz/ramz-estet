1) Vercel Environment Variables:
BOT_TOKEN=...
DATABASE_URL=...
ABLY_API_KEY=...
ADMIN_TELEGRAM_ID=...
ADMIN_SECRET=<openssl rand -hex 32>
STARTAPP_SECRET=<openssl rand -hex 32>
TELEGRAM_WEBHOOK_SECRET=<openssl rand -hex 32>
PUBLIC_BASE_URL=https://ramz-estet.vercel.app
BOT_USERNAME=ramzestetbot

ADMIN_SECRET va STARTAPP_SECRET bir xil bo‘lmasin.

2) Neon schema.sql ni bir marta Run qiling. Ayniqsa:

3) Deploy qiling.

4) Webhookni faqat header orqali o‘rnating:
POST https://ramz-estet.vercel.app/api/set-webhook
Header: x-admin-secret: ADMIN_SECRET

5) Mini App:
BotFather -> @ramzestetbot -> Mini App -> short name: giveaway
URL: https://ramz-estet.vercel.app

6) Guruh postida endi raw giveaway ID URLda bo‘lmaydi.
JOIN va WATCH linklari v1.<encrypted-token> ko‘rinishida bo‘ladi.

7) Eski linklar:
startapp=giveaway_123
startapp=join_123
startapp=watch_123
endi qabul qilinmaydi.

8) Eski manage linklar:
/manage.html?gid=123&token=ADMIN_SECRET
endi qabul qilinmaydi.
